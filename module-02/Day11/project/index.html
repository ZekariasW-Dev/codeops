About Habesha Market

Habesha Market is your trusted source for authentic Ethiopian products. Founded in 2020, we are dedicated to bringing the rich flavors and traditions of Ethiopia to homes around the world.

Our mission is to support Ethiopian farmers and artisans while providing our customers with the highest quality products.
